# Account research

Use this when the user wants background on a company or contact.

1. Delegate the lookup to the `researcher` subagent with the `task` tool. Give it the company or contact name and ask for deals, contacts, notes, open tasks and recent activity.
2. The researcher can only read. Do not ask it to change data.
3. From its answer, write a one-page briefing: who they are, open deals and stages, the last interactions, open tasks, and two or three talking points.
4. Say plainly what is missing from the data instead of guessing.
